TECNOVICE
TECH SUMMIT 2026

The Tecnnovice Tech Summit brings together developers, founders, students, and industry leaders who are passionate about building technology solutions for Africa. Every two years we gather in a different city to share knowledge, launch ideas, and strengthen the Cameroonian tech ecosystem.

In 2026 we return to Buea — the Silicon Mountain — to celebrate innovation, mentorship, and the next wave of African builders.

Please contact us per Email for any further questions about Tecnnovice Tech Summit 2026!
